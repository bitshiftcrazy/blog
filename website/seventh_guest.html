A Place at the Table

A little D&D text adventure about hospitality, mysteries, and labour laws.

To play, read the story and click one of the choices whenever they appear to decide what happens next.

A handful of people are gathered around a wooden table. Character sheets, pencils, colorful dice, and enough snacks to feed at least three times as many people as are present are scattered across its surface.

Someone is engaged in a rather heated argument about the specifics of druidic Wild Shape and animal companions.

As you approach, one of the players looks up.

"Oh, hello there!"

"General Kenobi," murmurs another player.

The original speaker gives them a brief, exasperated look before continuing to address you cheerfully.

"Are you here to play?"

Before you can even answer, they pull out an empty chair and push a bowl of crisps and crisp-dust-covered gummy bears towards you.

"Come in! There's always room for one more!"

At the head of the table, the DM looks up from behind her screen.

"Ah, wonderful! Another adventurer. Welcome! And just in time, too!"

She shuffles her notes, visibly delighted.

"You won't need a character sheet today. But you will need a twenty-sided die, a d20. Go fetch one if you haven't already. Or use this website if you don't have one: https://slyflourish.com/dice/

And when I ask you to roll, just tell me what you got. We'll trust you."

One of the players snickers.

"Speak for yourself."

The DM gives them a look, then turns back to you with a mischievous smile.

"Alright then. Are you ready for a little adventure?"

A) "Absolutely! Let’s play!" B) "Oh, not today, thank you."

The DM smiles.

"Of course! No pressure at all. You're always welcome to join us another time."

One of the players looks up from their character sheet.

"Wait! At least take some snacks for the road!"

They offer you a generous handful of gummy bears, now thoroughly coated in orange crisp dust.

You thank them and make your way towards the door.

Behind you, the familiar clicking of dice and cheerful arguments resume.

"Anyway, consider this, if druids are in Wild Shape..."

You close the door behind you.

And thus ends your first adventure.

You have successfully avoided all dangers, suffered no injuries, and acquired several questionable gummy bears.

A rather successful adventure, all things considered.

The End.
