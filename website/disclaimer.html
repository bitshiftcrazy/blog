When you visit this website

This website is hosted by Netlify. When you request a page, Netlify processes technical information such as your IP address, the requested URL, the time of access, and technical details of the request. Its infrastructure may retain server logs for operational and security purposes. I do not operate an additional web server or maintain my own visitor-identifying access logs.

The purpose of this processing is to deliver the site and keep it functioning and secure. The legal basis I rely on is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR: my legitimate interest in running a secure, accessible personal website. Netlify's own retention of technical logs is governed by its applicable policies; I cannot set their retention period myself.

Visitor statistics

I use Netlify Web Analytics to understand which pages are visited, where visitors broadly come from, and whether people encounter missing pages. It processes data from Netlify's CDN server logs rather than running tracking scripts in your browser. It does not use analytics cookies. Netlify counts unique visitors using IP addresses; the reports I access are aggregate statistics rather than individual browsing profiles.

I do not use Netlify Real User Monitoring or add my own client-side analytics JavaScript. My reason for reviewing these statistics is to understand and maintain the website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

The Lost + Found journal

If you leave an offering in the journal, the form sends your chosen keepsake, your message, and your name or pseudonym (if you choose to provide one) to Netlify Forms. Netlify also records submission metadata, including your IP address and the submission time. I cannot disable that IP-address collection in Netlify Forms.

Netlify checks submissions for spam using Akismet, provided by Automattic. The form also contains a hidden honeypot field to filter out some automated spam. I do not ask for an email address or require you to create an account.

I read and select entries manually. Not every entry will appear on the website; accepted entries may be edited, shortened, or reformatted before publication. If I publish your note, only the selected text and any name or pseudonym you supplied will appear, not your IP address or Netlify's submission metadata. Please do not submit sensitive personal details about yourself or other people.

Receiving, checking, moderating, and selectively publishing voluntary journal entries serves my legitimate interest in offering a small, curated guestbook (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR). Submission is entirely optional. You can contact me to object to processing or request removal of a published entry.

I delete submissions from my Netlify Forms inbox after reviewing them, and in any event within 30 days of receipt, including rejected, unreviewed, and spam-flagged entries. Published entries may stay on the website until I remove them or respond to a valid removal request. Removing an entry from the site cannot necessarily remove copies already made by third parties or retained in caches or public version histories.

Deleting a submission from the Netlify dashboard does not necessarily delete every technical log or backup maintained by a service provider at the same moment. Those records are subject to the provider's separate retention rules.
